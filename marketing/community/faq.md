# Seedee community FAQ

Status: proposed replies for review, not published support policy. Prepared by June, 2026-11-21 UTC. See the [claims ledger](../research/claims-check.md) for sources and release conditions.

### What is Seedee?

Seedee is being developed as a creative-agency planning product. The demo explores how a creative could shape an offer, organize a crew and projects, and prepare a company story with supporting evidence. This describes the intended demo scope; release must confirm the available workflow before sharing these replies.

### Is the demo using a live AI model?

The assigned demo uses deterministic built-in sample logic. A real-model connection is a later option, not a current capability promised by these drafts. Sample outputs should be reviewed as drafts.

### Does the crew mean I get human employees?

No. The demo's crew represents proposed agency roles. It does not mean Seedee has hired people or supplied a staffed agency.

### Will Seedee run my company or contact clients for me?

The demo scope covers internal planning and proposed work. It does not authorize sending client communications, publishing work, or making purchases on your behalf. You remain responsible for deciding what to use.

### Does investor readiness mean I will receive seed funding?

No. It refers to preparation and evidence organization. Any readiness rubric is a product heuristic, not an investor decision, valuation, approval, or probability of funding. Sample financial figures are fictional examples, not customer results or promised returns.

### Is Seedee suitable for every creative or business?

That has not been established. We welcome feedback on whether the proposed workflow fits your practice. These drafts make no claims about customer adoption, time saved, income, or business success.

### Is it free, and can I subscribe or invest now?

Public pricing, subscription availability, and an investment offering have not been confirmed for these drafts. The build uses no paid AI services; any payment feature is restricted to Stripe test mode. That is a demo boundary, not a promise of a permanently free product. Do not send money based on this preview.

### Where can I try it?

An approved public demo destination has not yet been supplied for this copy. We will use a release-verified link before publication; the repository address is not evidence that a hosted demo is live.

### What data should I use?

Use fictional, non-sensitive examples while evaluating the preview. These drafts do not establish privacy guarantees, production security certification, retention terms, or permission to upload client data. Do not share credentials or confidential client material in community replies.

### How should I give feedback?

Use the community's permitted feedback thread and describe the planning step that helped or confused you. General examples are enough. Participation is optional; we will not treat a question as permission to send a sales message.
