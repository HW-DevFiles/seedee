# Seedee flyer copy

Three editorial variations for Ali's 5-inch-wide × 4-inch-tall flyers. Draft for review; product claims follow the register in `messaging.md`. Each design should carry the disclosure as readable copy inside the trim, not in the bleed. Keep final destinations pending release verification.

Production handoff: 300 dpi print PDF with 1/8-inch bleed on every edge, plus a PNG for posting. Trim is 1500 × 1200 pixels at 300 dpi; the full bleed canvas is 1575 × 1275 pixels. These are layout specifications, not marketing statistics. Ali owns artwork, safe margins, export, and print verification.

## 1. Bold invitation

**Headline:** Your creative practice. A company taking shape.

**Body:** Explore a demo of your personal creative agency. Shape an offer, plan the work, and see what comes next.

**CTA:** Explore the Seedee demo.

**Disclosure:** Demo preview · Sample outputs

**Design fit:** Bold typographic invitation. Let the headline carry the composition; the body can wrap across a few short lines without adding copy.

## 2. Seed to studio

**Headline:** Start with your craft. Explore what comes next.

**Body:** Give your agency idea room to take shape. Explore a company story, a growth plan, and the evidence behind your next step.

**CTA:** Explore the Seedee demo.

**Disclosure:** Demo preview · Sample outputs · No funding guarantee

**Design fit:** Seed-to-studio illustration. Growth imagery expresses an aspiration, not a timeline or predicted business outcome.

## 3. Practical workflow

**Headline:** Give your next idea a working plan.

**Body:** Start with your creative practice. Explore how an agency could organize its crew, projects, and next steps.

**CTA:** Explore the Seedee demo.

**Disclosure:** Demo preview · Sample outputs

**Design fit:** Agency-workflow composition. Any depicted tasks, roles, or projects must be visibly illustrative, without fabricated client names, revenue, or completion claims.
