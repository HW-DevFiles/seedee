Editorial draft for owner review. This is messaging for the planned demo, not a record of released or tested capabilities. Confirm feature wording against the review build before publication. Nothing in this package authorizes public posting.

## Positioning

Seedee is a software factory for creatives: a place to create, nurture, and manage a personal creative agency, from an initial company idea toward organized work and evidence for future investor conversations.

In audience-facing introductions, explain “software factory” as a workspace for shaping an agency and its ways of working. The phrase must not imply autonomous production, completed client work, or a company operating without its owner's oversight.

## Audience

- Independent creatives exploring how their practice could become a company.
- Creative founders shaping an offer, a working process, and a plan for growth.
- Creatives considering whether outside capital fits their company, who need to understand and document the business first.

These are intended audiences, not claims about existing customers or demonstrated demand.

## Voice and tone

Speak as an organized, encouraging collaborator. Respect the reader's creative judgment. Use short sentences, concrete verbs, and examples clearly labeled as examples. Ambition belongs in the founder's goals; certainty belongs only in checked facts.

Lead with what a creative can explore. Explain the demo boundary near the first capability claim. Give the reader a useful next step rather than urgency or pressure. Prefer “review your agency draft” to “unleash your potential.”

For LinkedIn, give a practical use case and invite thoughtful feedback. For Twitter/X, keep one idea and one CTA per post; preserve the demo disclosure even when space is tight. For flyers, use one headline, two short body sentences at most, one CTA, and a legible disclosure.

## Taglines

1. Your creative practice. A company taking shape.
2. Build the business behind the work.
3. Give your next idea a working plan.
4. Shape your agency. Choose your next step.
5. Start with your craft. Explore what comes next.

## Words to use and avoid

| Use | Avoid |
| --- | --- |
| Personal creative agency, creative practice, company idea | Instant company, legally incorporated for you |
| Shape, explore, draft, review, nurture, manage | Fully autonomous, runs itself, done for you |
| Demo, sample output, built-in sample logic | Live AI agency, real-time intelligence, production-ready AI |
| Crew roles, proposed tasks, project plan | Employees hired, work delivered, clients acquired |
| Investor preparation, supporting evidence, assumptions | Guaranteed funding, investor-approved, fundable score |
| Growth plan, financial assumptions | Guaranteed returns, risk-free, proven profitability |

## Reusable disclosure and CTA

Short disclosure: **Demo preview · Sample outputs**

Expanded disclosure: **Seedee's demo uses built-in sample logic. Outputs are drafts for review. Investor preparation does not guarantee funding.**

Primary CTA: **Explore the Seedee demo.**

Only use a destination verified by the release owner. Until then, the CTA is proposed label text. Do not invent a launch date, domain, price, signup offer, QR destination, or availability claim.
