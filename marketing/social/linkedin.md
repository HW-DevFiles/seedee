Your creative practice. A company taking shape.

The idea is to give that agency a working plan. Start with your practice and goals. Explore an offer, a crew, and the work ahead. Bring the company story and its supporting evidence into focus as you prepare for conversations about growth, including possible seed investment.

## 3. Follow-up: how it works

### Post copy

What does “a personal creative agency” mean in Seedee?

Here's the journey we're designing:

1. Describe your creative practice and what you want to build.
2. Review a proposed agency direction, offer, and crew.
3. Organize projects and next steps around that direction.
4. Bring the company story and supporting evidence into focus for growth conversations.

The creative makes the decisions. A proposed plan becomes useful when you can inspect it, change it, and decide what to do next.

In this demo preview, AI steps use built-in sample logic. The sample outputs illustrate the workflow; they are not evidence of completed client work or customer traction.

Which step would you want to explore first?
